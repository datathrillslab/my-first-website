How to use

Erasmus Staff Mobility Network · University of Rijeka

The diagram

Each University of Rijeka faculty sits on the inner ring. The outer ring shows the destination universities their professors travelled to under Erasmus staff mobility.

Exploring

  • Tap a faculty (node or its entry in the left list) to fan out its professors and reveal where they went.
  • Tap a destination to see its name and which faculties have sent staff there.
  • Use the Sending Faculties list to open a faculty's professor dropdown; collapse it with the caret to keep scrolling.
  • Tap the background or Clear Selection to reset.
