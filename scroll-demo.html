Meet Commander Dave — the fearless astronaut who insists on bringing his lucky socks to every mission. He's trained for years, run thousands of simulations, and yet his biggest concern right now? Whether the onboard espresso machine actually works in zero gravity. One small sip for man, one giant latte for mankind.

Say hello to Mission Specialist Sara — pilot, engineer, and the only person on the crew who can parallel-park the lunar lander on the first try. She once fixed a life-support system with a paperclip and sheer determination. Houston doesn't have problems when Sara's on the line.

And here's their ride — a magnificent rocket that turns millions of pounds of fuel into pure, window-rattling thunder. It stands taller than a 30-story building and goes from zero to "see you later, Earth" in about eight minutes. Buckle up, it's going to be a loud one.
